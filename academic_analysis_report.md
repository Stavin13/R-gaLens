**Academic Analysis Report: Trend in Research Gaps and Shift in Discourse**

**Introduction**

The Music Academy Journals, spanning from 1930 to 2023, provide a valuable resource for analyzing the trend in research gaps and the shift in discourse regarding Indian music, particularly in the context of DESI components (Taalas, Raagas, Desi Prabandhas, etc.). This report aims to provide an in-depth analysis of the research gaps and the evolution of scholarly interest in Indian music over the years.

**Trend in Research Gaps (21st century vs earlier 20th century)**

A review of the journals reveals that the earlier 20th century (1930s-1950s) saw a significant focus on the theoretical and practical aspects of Indian music, with an emphasis on the publication of original articles, classical works, and authoritative reports on music conferences (Vol.13_1942.pdf, Page 94; Vol.41_1970.pdf, Page 2). In contrast, the 21st century has witnessed a shift towards more specialized and nuanced research, with a growing emphasis on the cultural and historical context of Indian music (Vol.80_2009.pdf, Page 7; Journal2020-22.pdf, Page 8).

The earlier 20th century saw a notable lack of research in areas such as the historical development of Indian music, the role of music in Indian culture, and the influence of Western music on Indian classical music. However, in recent years, there has been a growing interest in these areas, with scholars exploring the intersection of music and culture, the impact of globalization on Indian music, and the preservation of traditional music practices (Vol.28_1957.pdf, Page 81; Vol.73_2002.pdf, Page 21).

**Specific "Gaps" mentioned or implied by the shift in discourse**

1. **Historical development of Indian music**: The earlier 20th century saw a lack of research on the historical development of Indian music, with a focus on the theoretical and practical aspects. In recent years, there has been a growing interest in the historical context of Indian music, with scholars exploring the evolution of Indian music and its relationship to Indian culture (Vol.19_1948.pdf, Page 3).
2. **Role of music in Indian culture**: The earlier 20th century saw a lack of research on the role of music in Indian culture, with a focus on the technical aspects of music. In recent years, there has been a growing interest in the cultural significance of music in Indian society, with scholars exploring the intersection of music and culture (Vol.37_1966.pdf, Page 2).
3. **Influence of Western music on Indian classical music**: The earlier 20th century saw a lack of research on the influence of Western music on Indian classical music, with a focus on the preservation of traditional music practices. In recent years, there has been a growing interest in the impact of globalization on Indian music, with scholars exploring the intersection of Indian and Western music traditions (Vol.28_1957.pdf, Page 86).

**Major Areas of Interest for Scholars over Time**

**Definition of "Scholarly Interest"**

The definition of "scholarly interest" has undergone a significant shift over the years. In the earlier 20th century, scholarly interest was focused on the technical aspects of music, with an emphasis on the publication of original articles and classical works. In recent years, scholarly interest has expanded to include the cultural and historical context of Indian music, with a growing emphasis on the preservation of traditional music practices and the role of music in Indian culture.

**Marga vs Desi**

The concept of Marga (classical) and Desi (folk) music has been a persistent theme throughout the journals. The earlier 20th century saw a focus on the classical traditions of Indian music, with a emphasis on the publication of original articles and classical works (Vol.1_1930.pdf, Page 2). In recent years, there has been a growing interest in the folk music traditions of India, with scholars exploring the intersection of classical and folk music traditions (Vol.73_2002.pdf, Page 21).
